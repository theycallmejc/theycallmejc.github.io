ECS vs EKS

ECS Fargate fits lower container-operational overhead. EKS is appropriate when Kubernetes portability and ecosystem requirements are explicit.

ALB vs NLB

ALB supports HTTP/S routing, TLS and path rules; NLB fits connection-level, high-throughput, or static-IP constraints.

Blue/green vs rolling

Blue/green isolates the candidate and clarifies rollback; it trades additional capacity for safer traffic transitions.
